10. EXPLANATION
============================================================
The Logistic Regression model predicted Buy / Not Buy correctly for 86.0% of the 100 test customers.
Product price has the strongest negative effect: expensive products are much less likely to be bought, while higher income and more previous purchases raise the chance of buying; older customers are slightly less likely to buy.
The confusion matrix shows 14 mistakes (7 false Buy, 7 missed buyers); 10 of them had a predicted probability between 0.2 and 0.8, i.e. borderline customers.
Because the classes are balanced and errors are split fairly evenly, accuracy is a fair summary here, and the model is useful for deciding which customers to target with offers.
